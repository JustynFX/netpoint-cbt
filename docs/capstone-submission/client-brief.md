# Client Brief: NetPoint CBT & Cyber Café

**Client:** Mr. Edosa Ekhator  
**Business:** NetPoint CBT & Cyber Café  
**Service area:** Ugbowo, near UNIBEN Main Gate, Benin City, Edo State

## The problem

During JAMB and Post-UTME periods, students can spend time in registration queues and make repeated late-night calls or WhatsApp enquiries about documents, profile codes, biometrics, printing and opening hours. The client estimates that the centre may serve around **300 students per month at peak**. This is a planning estimate and should be confirmed with the client before being presented as audited business data.

## Who the site serves

UTME and Direct Entry candidates, UNIBEN students, and Benin City residents who need legitimate exam-registration assistance, printing, photocopying, binding, document typing or online-portal support.

## Common student questions

1. Where is the centre, and how do I find it from UNIBEN Main Gate?
2. What are the opening hours, including Sundays and peak registration periods?
3. What should I bring for JAMB or Post-UTME registration?
4. How do I handle a missing profile code or a biometric problem?
5. Which printing, photocopying, scanning and binding services are available?
6. Can I book a visit or ask for help with an online portal?
7. How much will a particular service cost? (The current price must be confirmed by the centre.)
8. Can anyone guarantee an exam score or admission? (No.)

## The Never Rule

NetPoint CBT must never promise, predict or imply a guaranteed JAMB score, Post-UTME score, exam result or university admission. JAMB, WAEC and UNIBEN determine official results and admission decisions.

## What we built

- A mobile-first, one-page website for NetPoint CBT with service information, a JAMB preparation checklist, opening hours, Ugbowo location details, concept-image showcase and enquiry form.
- A native enquiry API that validates enquiries and can classify them as HOT, WARM or CHECK, then independently attempts configured sheet logging, email and Telegram notifications.
- A PIN-protected owner dashboard for enquiry counts, service breakdown and searchable enquiry records.
- A Botpress webchat integration for customer questions and direction to the enquiry form.

## Contact and service expectations

The site lists Monday to Saturday, 7:30 AM–8:00 PM, and Sunday closure. Peak-season extensions and exact visit slots must be confirmed by the centre. No prices, current JAMB deadlines or guaranteed outcomes are invented by the site or chatbot.
