# NetPoint CBT Knowledge Base

## Business overview

NetPoint CBT & Cyber Café is managed by Mr. Edosa Ekhator in Ugbowo, near UNIBEN Main Gate, Benin City, Edo State. It supports students and residents with registration guidance, printing and document services. It does not control official JAMB, WAEC or UNIBEN systems, results or admission decisions.

## Frequently asked questions

### What services does NetPoint CBT provide?

The centre assists with JAMB, Direct Entry and Post-UTME registration steps; printing, photocopying, scanning and project binding; document typing; and guided online applications. Confirm the exact service and current price with the centre before visiting.

### Where is NetPoint CBT?

NetPoint CBT is in Ugbowo, near UNIBEN Main Gate, Benin City, Edo State. Ask through the website enquiry form to confirm the best directions or a nearby landmark before travelling.

### What are the opening hours?

The website lists Monday to Saturday, 7:30 AM to 8:00 PM, and Sunday closure. Online enquiries can be submitted at any time. Ask the centre to confirm any extended peak-season hours and visit availability.

### What should I prepare for JAMB registration?

Prepare your NIN details, the personal email address and phone number you can access, your O'Level result details or awaiting-result status, and your preferred institution, course and subject combination. Check current requirements through official JAMB guidance before your visit. Do not send sensitive information in this chat.

### What if my JAMB profile code has not arrived?

The chatbot cannot inspect or repair a JAMB account. Do not post your full NIN, OTP, password or profile code here. Use the website's Enquire / Book Slot form to ask for personal help from the NetPoint team, and confirm official steps with JAMB.

### Can NetPoint help with a biometric or portal problem?

The centre can provide registration and technical support, but the chatbot cannot access your account or promise that a portal issue will be resolved. Use the enquiry form to ask the team to review the issue. Never share passwords, OTPs or payment details in chat.

### Can I book a registration visit?

Submit the Enquire / Book Slot form with your preferred day, time and service. A request is not a confirmed appointment; wait for the centre to reply and confirm availability.

### How much does printing, binding or registration cost?

Prices depend on the service and job details and are not listed in this knowledge base. Ask through the Enquire / Book Slot form for a current quote before placing an order.

### Can NetPoint guarantee my JAMB score, exam result or admission?

No. NetPoint CBT cannot guarantee or predict any score, result or admission. JAMB, WAEC and UNIBEN make official result and admission decisions. NetPoint can only assist with legitimate registration, document and portal steps.

### How do I contact the team?

Use the Enquire / Book Slot form on the website. Include the service you need and a preferred visit time. Do not include passwords, OTPs, full NIN details or payment credentials.
